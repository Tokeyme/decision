Decision Maker

Decision Maker is a calm management sim: in 1994 you take over a small trucking company in Stuttgart, Germany, and run it from the office. You take on orders, send out trucks, hire drivers, expand your fleet and keep an eye on your cash — you never sit behind the wheel. A free browser game, no sign-up.
